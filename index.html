What I Do

Voice AI Pipelines

Building production voice agents end-to-end: speech-to-text, LLM reasoning, and text-to-speech. I focus on the details that decide whether users stay, such as latency, conversation quality, and multilingual performance.

Multilingual & Arabic NLP

Native Arabic speaker working on models and data for African and Arabic-speaking markets. Six years of building and auditing multilingual datasets means I know where language models break, and how to measure it.

ML Systems & Data Engineering

From raw, messy data to deployed models: dataset construction, quality control, LLM-powered extraction tools, and full-stack ML services like PRO-TEST, an ensemble model with a REST API, CI/CD, and monitoring.

AI/LLM-Powered Research Automation

Develop custom, Python-based AI and computational tools to automate and streamline multi-step academic research workflows.
